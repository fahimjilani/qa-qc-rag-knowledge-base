DOCUMENT TITLE: NCR Closure and Verification Criteria
DOCUMENT ID: QA-SOP-008
VERSION: 1.0
STATUS: Approved for Training / Demo Use
PROCESS OWNER: Quality Assurance
REVIEW FREQUENCY: Annual

1. PURPOSE

This procedure defines the minimum criteria for verifying, approving, and closing Non-Conformance Reports (NCRs).

The objective is to ensure that an NCR is closed only after required actions are completed, evidence is available, and effectiveness has been verified where applicable.

2. SCOPE

This procedure applies to:

- Minor NCRs
- Major NCRs
- Audit findings
- Document-control NCRs
- Inspection-related NCRs
- Repeated quality failures
- CAPA-linked NCRs
- Overdue NCRs

3. GENERAL CLOSURE RULE

An NCR must not be closed simply because the immediate issue was corrected.

Closure requires verification that:

- Required correction is complete
- Corrective actions are complete
- Required evidence is available
- Responsible owner has completed assigned actions
- Required approvals are complete
- Effectiveness verification is completed where required
- No mandatory action remains open

4. CORRECTION VS CORRECTIVE ACTION

Correction:
Immediate action taken to fix the identified problem.

Examples:

- Replace obsolete form
- Correct inaccurate record
- Repeat missed inspection
- Restore missing approval evidence where valid

Corrective Action:
Action taken to remove the root cause and prevent recurrence.

Examples:

- Revise SOP
- Add system control
- Introduce approval validation
- Retrain personnel
- Improve checklist
- Modify workflow

Correction alone may not be sufficient for closure when root cause action is required.

5. MINOR NCR CLOSURE

A Minor NCR may be closed when:

- Required correction is completed
- Supporting evidence is available
- Assigned action is complete
- No significant residual risk remains
- QA/QC verification is complete where required

Root cause analysis may not be required for every isolated Minor NCR.

6. MAJOR NCR CLOSURE

A Major NCR must not be closed until:

- Containment is complete
- Root cause analysis is completed
- Corrective action is implemented
- Required CAPA is initiated or completed
- Supporting evidence is available
- QA Management approval is obtained
- Effectiveness verification is completed
- Significant residual risk is addressed

7. EFFECTIVENESS VERIFICATION

Effectiveness verification is mandatory for:

- Major NCRs
- Repeated Minor NCRs
- CAPA-related findings
- Systemic failures
- Previous ineffective corrective actions

Verification may include:

- Follow-up audit
- Sample review
- Repeat inspection
- Process observation
- System validation
- Record verification
- Trend analysis
- Control testing

8. EFFECTIVENESS CRITERIA

Corrective action may be considered effective when:

- Root cause is addressed
- Issue has not recurred during the verification period
- New control works as intended
- Required records are available
- No unacceptable residual risk remains
- Process owner is following the revised control

9. INEFFECTIVE ACTION

Corrective action must be considered ineffective when:

- Same issue recurs
- Required action was not fully implemented
- Root cause remains unresolved
- Evidence is insufficient
- New control is not operating as intended
- Similar failure appears in another affected area

An ineffective corrective action must be escalated.

10. CLOSURE EVIDENCE

Acceptable closure evidence may include:

- Revised approved SOP
- Completed training records
- Updated system configuration
- Approved workflow change
- Corrected quality record
- Reinspection result
- Follow-up audit report
- CAPA completion record
- Management approval
- Effectiveness review report

Verbal confirmation alone is not sufficient where documented evidence is required.

11. CLOSURE APPROVAL

Minor NCR:
May be closed by authorized QA/QC personnel, subject to applicable process rules.

Major NCR:
Requires QA Management closure approval.

CAPA-linked NCR:
Must remain linked to CAPA status.

The NCR should not be closed if mandatory CAPA actions remain unresolved.

12. OVERDUE NCR

An NCR is overdue when:

- Due date has passed
- Required actions remain incomplete

Overdue Minor NCR:
Requires follow-up with Process Owner.

Overdue Major NCR:
Requires escalation to QA Management.

Repeated overdue NCRs may indicate systemic control weakness.

13. REOPENING AN NCR

A closed NCR should be reopened when:

- Same issue recurs
- Closure evidence is later found incomplete
- Corrective action proves ineffective
- Required action was incorrectly marked complete
- New evidence changes the original conclusion

14. RESIDUAL RISK

Before closure, any remaining risk should be evaluated.

An NCR must not be closed when:

- Significant customer risk remains
- Regulatory or contractual risk remains unresolved
- Safety risk remains unacceptable
- Critical control failure remains active
- Required approval remains missing

15. CLOSURE STATUS

Recommended statuses include:

- Open
- Under Investigation
- Action in Progress
- Pending Verification
- Pending Approval
- Closed
- Overdue
- Reopened

16. CLOSURE CHECKLIST

Before closing an NCR, confirm:

- Requirement identified
- Classification confirmed
- Correction complete
- Root cause completed where required
- Corrective action completed
- CAPA linked where required
- Evidence available
- Owner action completed
- Effectiveness verified where required
- Approval completed
- No mandatory action remains open

17. MANUAL QA REVIEW RULE

Manual QA Review is required when:

- Closure evidence is incomplete
- Effectiveness cannot be demonstrated
- Required approval is missing
- CAPA status is unclear
- Residual risk cannot be assessed
- User-provided information conflicts with available records

The RAG Copilot must not assume that an NCR is closed simply because the user states that work was completed.

18. RAG COPILOT DECISION RULE

For closure-related questions, the QA/QC RAG Copilot must:

- Identify whether closure criteria are satisfied.
- Identify missing evidence or actions.
- Determine whether effectiveness verification is required.
- Determine whether management approval is required.
- Recommend reopening where evidence shows ineffective action.
- Identify the source document used.
- Never invent completion evidence, approvals, dates, or effectiveness results.

Possible conclusions include:

READY FOR CLOSURE

NOT READY FOR CLOSURE

ESCALATION REQUIRED

MANUAL QA REVIEW REQUIRED

END OF DOCUMENT
