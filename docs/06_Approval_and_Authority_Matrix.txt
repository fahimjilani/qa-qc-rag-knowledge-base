DOCUMENT TITLE: Approval and Authority Matrix
DOCUMENT ID: QA-SOP-006
VERSION: 1.0
STATUS: Approved for Training / Demo Use
PROCESS OWNER: Quality Assurance
REVIEW FREQUENCY: Annual

1. PURPOSE

This document defines minimum approval and authority requirements for QA/QC decisions, document changes, NCR classification, CAPA, inspection release, and exceptional quality decisions.

The objective is to ensure that decisions are made only by authorized roles.

2. GENERAL RULE

No employee may approve a quality decision outside their authorized responsibility.

Where a required approval is missing, the activity must not be treated as fully approved.

Unauthorized approval must be evaluated as a quality-control failure.

3. APPROVAL LEVELS

LEVEL 1 – PROCESS OWNER

Typical authority:

- Routine process corrections
- Minor documentation updates
- Isolated Minor NCR actions
- Standard record completion
- Low-risk rework

LEVEL 2 – QA/QC REPRESENTATIVE

Typical authority:

- QA/QC verification
- Minor NCR validation
- Inspection acceptance
- Reinspection approval
- Controlled-document review
- Evidence verification

LEVEL 3 – QA MANAGER

Typical authority:

- Major NCR confirmation
- CAPA approval
- Major document-control deviations
- Significant quality-risk decisions
- Overdue Major NCR escalation
- Conditional acceptance of higher-risk cases

LEVEL 4 – FUNCTIONAL / SENIOR MANAGEMENT

Typical authority:

- High business-impact decisions
- Significant customer commitments
- Multi-department quality failures
- Major contractual exposure
- Significant process exceptions

LEVEL 5 – EXECUTIVE / REGULATORY AUTHORITY

Required only where applicable for:

- Regulatory reporting
- Critical legal exposure
- Severe safety risk
- Exceptional business-risk acceptance

4. DOCUMENT APPROVAL

Controlled documents require approval before operational use.

Typical approval requirements:

Work Instruction:
- Process Owner review
- QA/QC approval where quality controls are affected

SOP:
- Process Owner
- QA/QC review
- Required management approval

Quality Policy:
- QA Management
- Senior Management

Controlled Form:
- Process Owner
- QA/QC approval

Unapproved drafts must not be used operationally.

5. NCR APPROVAL

COMPLIANT:
No NCR approval required.

OBSERVATION:
May be recorded by auditor, QA/QC staff, or authorized reviewer.

MINOR NCR:
May be validated by QA/QC Representative or QA Manager.

MAJOR NCR:
Must be confirmed or approved by QA Management.

If classification is uncertain, the case must be escalated for manual QA review.

6. CAPA APPROVAL

CAPA associated with a Major NCR requires QA Management approval.

Additional Functional or Senior Management approval may be required when:

- Multiple departments are affected
- Significant cost exists
- Customer commitment is affected
- Contractual exposure exists
- Significant system change is required

7. INSPECTION AND RELEASE AUTHORITY

Routine acceptance may be performed by an authorized QA/QC inspector.

The following require higher approval:

- Conditional acceptance
- Critical defect disposition
- Release with approved deviation
- Release after Major NCR
- Exception to mandatory acceptance criteria

Unauthorized final release is a significant control failure.

8. DEVIATION APPROVAL

Any temporary deviation from an approved procedure must:

- Be documented
- Have defined scope
- Have defined duration
- Include risk assessment where required
- Receive appropriate approval before use

A deviation must not become a permanent unofficial process.

9. DOCUMENT CHANGE AUTHORITY

Controlled-document changes require:

- Document owner
- Required technical reviewer
- QA/QC review where applicable
- Authorized approver

An employee must not change mandatory controls, approval sections, or acceptance criteria without authorized approval.

10. MAJOR NCR ESCALATION

Major NCRs must be escalated to QA Management.

Additional escalation is required when:

- Customer impact exists
- Regulatory risk exists
- Contractual risk exists
- Safety risk exists
- Multiple departments are affected
- Senior management decision is required
- Business continuity may be affected

11. OVERDUE ACTION AUTHORITY

Overdue Minor NCR:
- Process Owner
- QA/QC follow-up

Overdue Major NCR:
- QA Manager escalation required

Overdue CAPA:
- QA Manager review required

Repeated overdue actions may require Functional Management escalation.

12. AUTHORITY CONFLICT

A person should not approve their own work where independent verification is required.

Where independence is required, approval must be obtained from another authorized person.

13. MISSING APPROVAL

Missing required approval must be evaluated based on risk and impact.

MINOR NCR may apply when:

- Approval was delayed but completed before final use or release
- No mandatory control was bypassed
- No significant quality impact occurred

MAJOR NCR may apply when:

- Mandatory approval was completely bypassed
- Operational use occurred before authorization
- Customer, contractual, regulatory, or safety risk exists
- Multiple records or outputs are affected

Final classification must follow the Non-Conformance Procedure and NCR Severity Classification Matrix.

14. EMERGENCY DECISIONS

Emergency quality decisions must still be traceable.

Where normal approval cannot be obtained:

- Authorized emergency approver must be identified
- Reason must be documented
- Scope must be limited
- Decision must be reviewed after the event
- Required permanent approval must be completed where applicable

Emergency conditions do not automatically remove quality-control requirements.

15. APPROVAL EVIDENCE

Acceptable evidence may include:

- Electronic approval record
- Approved workflow history
- Signed controlled document
- Authorized system approval
- Formal quality-record approval

Informal verbal approval alone is insufficient where documented approval is mandatory.

16. RAG COPILOT DECISION RULE

The QA/QC RAG Copilot must:

- Identify the decision requiring approval.
- Determine the minimum authority level supported by this document.
- Check whether the described approval satisfies the requirement.
- Never assume that an approval exists.
- Escalate Major NCR, CAPA, critical release, and high-risk exception decisions appropriately.
- Cross-reference the Non-Conformance Procedure when required approval was bypassed.
- Identify the source document used.

If the available evidence does not clearly establish who approved the action or whether the approver had authority, respond:

MANUAL QA REVIEW REQUIRED
